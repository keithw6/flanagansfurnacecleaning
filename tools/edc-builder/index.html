Pick your carry

Every item is drawn to true relative scale, so the board shows how the kit actually sizes up against itself. Prices and weights are published-spec estimates — check the maker's page before you buy anything.

Add a product you found

Paste a link, fill in the cost and weight, pick a category. It joins the catalogue and stays in this browser.

The page itself cannot be read from here — another site will not allow it — so the cost and weight are yours to fill in. The brand and product name are guessed from the address.

Weight in ounces? Type it in grams — 1 oz is 28.3 g. The two size figures are the footprint as the thing lies on a board, and they are what puts it at the right size next to everything else.

Picture

Without a picture it is drawn in the same style as everything else, at the size you gave. A cut-out image on a white or transparent background sits best.

Your products

Kept in this browser. They travel in a share link, but their pictures do not — export the JSON to move those to another machine.

    Generate one image per product, cut the background out, save each as media/stills/<id>.png, and add it to media/manifest.js. A generated image is a likeness, not a photograph of the real article, and the app labels it that way.

    Real pictures

    Drop image files here and they are matched to products by filename — bm535.png, or benchmade-bugout-535.png. Any product can take one, catalogue or your own. Or use Photo on a single card.

    Straight off a maker’s site

    On the product page, right-click the photo and Copy image. Come back, pick the product below, and press Ctrl+V (⌘+V on a Mac). The picture never goes through another site, so it works where an image address does not.

    A whole list at once

    One product per line: the product id, a space, then the address of the image. Whether this works depends on the site holding the images allowing another page to read them — many do, some do not, and the report below says which lines failed and why.

    A photo on a plain backdrop comes out cut out and trimmed to its own edges, which is what lets the board draw it at the product’s real size. Turn both off for an image that is already cut out.

    Browser storage holds a handful. For a whole catalogue’s worth, save the manifest and drop it in tools/edc-builder/media/ — then the pictures survive a cleared browser and everyone you share a board with sees them.
