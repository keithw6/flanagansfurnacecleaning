The Straight Answer About Duct Sanitizing

Sanitizing is the most oversold service in this industry. The pitch is familiar: a technician shows you a photo of a mould-covered duct, suggests it might be yours, and quotes a few hundred dollars to treat it. Sometimes the photo is not even from your house.

Here is what is actually true. Dust in ductwork is dust. It is skin cells, fabric fibre, pet dander, soil tracked in on shoes and fine particulate from outside. It is not alive, it does not grow, and spraying a disinfectant on it accomplishes nothing that vacuuming it out does not accomplish better.

Mould needs moisture. Calgary's climate is dry, and a normal residential duct system running on a dry furnace has nowhere near the humidity to support mould growth. Most Calgary homes will never need their ducts sanitized, and if someone tells you otherwise without pointing at an actual moisture source, be sceptical.

What we will always do first is clean the system properly. If there is something biological in there, removing it mechanically is the fix. Sanitizing is a follow-up step for specific situations, not a substitute for the cleaning and not an automatic addition to it.

When Sanitizing Is Genuinely Warranted

There are real cases. We do this work when one applies.

Water damage

A burst pipe, a flooded basement, a failed hot water tank or sewer backup that reached the ductwork. Wet duct plus organic dust is the one combination that genuinely grows something, and the ducts need treating after they are dried and cleaned.

Confirmed moisture in the system

A cracked condensate line, an over-humidified home, or an evaporator coil that has been sitting wet. If we find standing moisture or visible growth on inspection, we will show it to you before recommending anything.

Rodents or birds

Mice in the ductwork, or a bird that got into an exhaust run and died there. Droppings and nesting material are a legitimate biological contamination and treating after removal is sensible.

Smoke damage

After a house fire, or heavy wildfire smoke infiltration during a bad Alberta summer. Smoke residue coats duct walls and re-releases odour every time the blower runs. Cleaning removes most of it, treatment handles the rest.

Heavy odour from a previous owner

Long-term indoor smoking, or a home with significant pet odour that has soaked into the system. New owners often want this dealt with, and it is a reasonable want.

Some medical situations

Occasionally a household with someone immunocompromised is advised to have it done. If a doctor has told you to, we will do it without argument.

When We Will Tell You Not To Bother

  • An ordinary dusty system with no moisture history. Cleaning is the answer, not chemicals.
  • A new build with construction dust. That is drywall, not biology.
  • General allergy concerns. Removing the dust helps; disinfecting it does not. See indoor air quality for what actually moves the needle.
  • Because another company said you had mould without showing you any.
  • As an annual routine. There is no reason to disinfect a dry duct system every year.
  • Fogging deodorizers to make the house smell fresh. That masks rather than removes, and the smell comes back.

If you have been quoted sanitizing by someone else and want a second opinion, call and describe what they told you. We will give you an honest read even if the answer is that you do not need us either.

How We Do It When It Is Needed

  1. Clean first, always

    A full mechanical furnace and duct cleaning comes first. Treating a dirty system is pointless because the product cannot reach the surface through a layer of dust.

  2. Identify and fix the source

    If there is moisture, the moisture has to be dealt with or the problem returns. We will tell you what needs fixing even when it is not work we do.

  3. Apply through the system

    Applied through the ductwork so it reaches the surfaces, with the product and coverage matched to the actual problem.

  4. Ventilate before reoccupancy

    We will tell you how long to keep the house aired out and when it is fine for children and pets to be back in normally.

Quoted separately from the cleaning, always, so you can see exactly what each part costs and decline the second part if you want to. See how we price things.
