The Upsells We Do Not Use

Duct cleaning has a reputation problem in Calgary and it was earned honestly. These are the specific tactics people run into, and the reason each one exists.

The advertised special that is not the price

A very low headline figure gets the booking. On arrival it turns out to cover the main trunk only, or the first six vents, and the real number is several times higher. By then there is a crew in your basement and saying no feels awkward.

Us: the number quoted on the phone is the whole job.

The per-vent surcharge

A price is quoted for a nominal vent count, then every vent above that is charged individually. Since almost nobody knows their real vent count, almost every job comes in over.

Us: if we misjudge the vent count, that is our estimating error, not a line on your invoice.

The mould photograph

A technician produces a photo of badly contaminated ductwork and implies it is yours, then quotes a sanitizing treatment. Sometimes the photo is a stock image.

Us: if we find something, we show you it in your own house. When sanitizing is actually warranted is a short and specific list.

The mandatory add-on

Sanitizing, deodorizing or an antimicrobial fog presented as a required part of the service rather than an option, and only itemised once the invoice appears.

Us: add-ons are quoted separately, in advance, and you can say no without any fuss.

The access fee

An additional charge because the furnace is in a tight closet, the vents are behind furniture, or the house has more than one storey. All of which were knowable before arriving.

Us: no access fees. We deal with the house as it is.

The scare referral

A cleaning turns into an alarming report about your heat exchanger and a same-day quote for a new furnace, often from an affiliated company.

Us: we do not sell furnaces. If we see something concerning we tell you plainly and you can get it assessed by whoever you like.

Payment and Booking

Payment is due on completion. Cash, debit, credit card and e-transfer are all fine. You get an invoice with the work itemised.

Booking is by phone or through the contact form. Have the approximate square footage, the number of storeys, whether the basement is developed, and a rough vent count if you can manage it. If you cannot, the square footage and storeys are enough to get close.

If your home does not need cleaning yet, we will tell you on that call rather than booking you in. It happens regularly and it is the main reason people ring us back three years later.
