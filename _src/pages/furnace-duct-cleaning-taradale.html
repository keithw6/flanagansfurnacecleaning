Furnace and Duct Cleaning for Taradale Homes

Taradale filled in between roughly 1997 and 2008, west of 68 Street NE around Taradale Drive and Taracove, with the Genesis Centre and the Saddletowne LRT station on its doorstep. It came slightly ahead of Saddle Ridge in the same wave of northeast expansion, so the housing here is on average a few years older.

Those few years matter. A system built in 2000 has had a quarter of a century of continuous winters, and Calgary furnaces run seven months a year. That is a lot of blower hours and a lot of dust pulled through the same pipe.

Most Taradale homes are two-storey detached with an attached or rear-lane garage, and the majority have a developed basement, added either at possession or in the years after. That basement development is the thing that most often prompts the call, because drywall sanding with the furnace running puts an enormous quantity of fine dust into the return side in a couple of days.

What We Usually Find in Taradale

Heavily loaded return side

The cold air returns and the basement return in particular carry the most. Returns pull everything the house produces toward the furnace, so they load faster than the supply side and are the part most often skipped by a cheap cleaning.

Secondary suite ductwork

Taradale has a high rate of suited basements. Branch lines added after the original build, sometimes flex, sometimes tapped into the existing trunk, are frequently the dirtiest runs in the house because they were installed into a system already in service. We clean them like everything else, at no additional charge.

Higher occupancy

Multi-generational households are common here. More people means more cooking, laundry and foot traffic, and homes where someone cooks daily at high heat push a fine grease aerosol into the return that holds onto everything landing on it. Those homes suit a three year cycle rather than five.

Furred blower wheels

Twenty-plus years of pet hair and fine dust packed between the fins. It reduces airflow substantially and it is the part of the system nobody has ever laid eyes on. Seeing yours is usually the moment the job justifies itself.

Replacement furnaces in old ductwork

Original units are largely gone by now. Where a modern high-efficiency unit has been fitted into ductwork sized for the older furnace, airflow behaviour changes, and a partially loaded system shows up sooner than it used to.

Untouched exhaust runs

Bathroom and kitchen exhaust ducts in homes of this age have very often never been cleaned. Same for the dryer vent, which is the one that actually matters for fire risk.

Landlords and Suited Homes

With as many suited basements as Taradale has, a fair share of our work here is for owners renting out the lower level. Two things worth knowing.

First, the suite ductwork is part of the same system and we clean it as part of the same flat price. There is no separate charge for the lower unit being harder to reach or for having to coordinate access with a tenant.

Second, dryer vent cleaning is worth doing between tenancies. It takes under an hour, costs very little as an add-on, and matters considerably if there is ever an insurance question about a laundry fire. If the suite has its own dryer, that is two vents rather than one.
