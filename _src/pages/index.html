Professional Furnace Cleaning in Calgary

A furnace cleaning is not just a vacuum stuck in the return vent for ten minutes. Your furnace has a blower assembly, a heat exchanger surface, a burner compartment and a filter rack, and every one of them collects the fine grey dust that Calgary homes produce all winter. When that dust builds up, the blower moves less air, the furnace runs longer to hit the same temperature, and the whole system works harder than it needs to.

We clean the furnace itself and the duct system it feeds, because cleaning one without the other just moves the dust around. That means pulling the blower where the unit allows it, cleaning the burner compartment, brushing and vacuuming the supply trunk and every branch line, and putting a fresh filter in before we leave.

What a furnace cleaning includes

  • Blower compartment and blower wheel cleaned
  • Burner compartment vacuumed out
  • Heat exchanger surfaces cleaned and inspected visually
  • Supply trunk and every branch line brushed and vacuumed
  • All supply and return registers pulled and cleaned
  • Cold air return cleaned back to the furnace
  • New standard filter installed
  • Before and after look inside the system, with you standing there

Why Calgary Homes Need Regular Furnace Cleaning

Calgary is harder on a forced-air system than most Canadian cities, and it comes down to how long the furnace runs and what it is pulling in while it does.

A seven-month heating season

Furnaces here run from roughly late September through April, and in a cold snap they run nearly continuously. That is thousands more blower hours per year than a furnace in Vancouver or Toronto sees, and every one of those hours pulls dust through the system.

Chinooks and constant cycling

A chinook can swing the temperature thirty degrees in a day. Your furnace responds by cycling on and off far more often than a steady climate would demand, and each start-up pulls a fresh slug of settled dust off the duct walls and pushes it into the house.

Dry prairie air and fine dust

Low humidity keeps dust airborne instead of letting it settle, so more of it ends up circulating and collecting inside the ductwork. Add spring wind off open land east and north of the city and there is a steady supply of it.

New construction on every edge

Calgary has been building continuously for twenty years. Homes in Redstone, Cornerstone, Mahogany and Seton get drywall dust and sawdust in the ducts during construction, and the builder walkthrough rarely deals with it properly.

Finished basements and renovations

Developing a basement puts an enormous amount of drywall dust into the return system, especially if the furnace ran during the work. This is the single most common reason people in established NE communities call us.

Pets, and a lot of them

Pet hair and dander end up in the return ducts and wrap themselves around the blower wheel. A furred blower wheel moves noticeably less air, and most people have never seen theirs.

Most Calgary homes are well served by a cleaning every three to five years. Homes with pets, recent renovations, allergy sufferers or a newly built house benefit from the shorter end of that range. We will tell you honestly which one you are, and we would rather you waited two more years than paid us for work you did not need.

Our Furnace Cleaning Process

Every job runs the same way, whether it is a bungalow in Coventry Hills or a two-storey in Cranston.

  1. We quote before we arrive

    Tell us the size of the home and roughly how many vents it has and we will give you a flat price on the phone. That number does not move once we are in the driveway.

  2. We protect the house first

    Drop sheets at the door, boots off or covered, and equipment routed so it is not dragging across your floors.

  3. Furnace unit, then ductwork

    The blower and burner compartment get cleaned first, then we work the duct system under negative pressure, brushing each branch line back toward the trunk so the debris has somewhere to go.

  4. We show you the result

    Before we pack up, you get a look inside the system and a look at what came out of it. No photographs of somebody else's ductwork, no upsell pitch. Then a fresh filter goes in.

Supply side in grey, return side in light grey. Both get cleaned. A cleaning that only touches the supply registers leaves half the system dirty.

Furnace Cleaning and Duct Cleaning Together

Most of our calls end up being both. The furnace and the ductwork are one connected system, and cleaning the unit while leaving the ducts full of dust means the first time the blower kicks on it pulls that dust straight back through. If you are booking a furnace cleaning, ask about the combined price for duct cleaning in Calgary at the same visit. It is substantially cheaper than two separate trips, and it is the only way to actually get the system clean.
