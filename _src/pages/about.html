Owner-Operated, and That Is the Point

The person who quotes your job is the person who shows up to do it. There is no call centre, no sales representative who visits ahead of the crew, and no crew of subcontractors working to a daily target set by somebody else.

That structure is the reason the pricing works the way it does. When the person quoting is the person doing the work, there is no incentive to lowball on the phone and make it up on site, because there is nobody to hand the awkward conversation to. If we misjudge a house, we wear it.

It is also the reason nothing gets rushed. A company running three crews with five jobs each per day has to move. We would rather do fewer houses properly and have people call us again in four years.

28 Years in Calgary

BBB Accredited since 1998, which means a very long stretch of Calgary homeowners having somewhere to complain if the work was not right. Twenty-eight years is enough time to have cleaned systems in nearly every kind of house this city has built.

The inner-city stock

Post-war bungalows and 1960s and 70s builds with oversized trunks, original octopus layouts, and occasionally asbestos-wrapped sections that need identifying and leaving alone rather than disturbing.

The 1980s to 2000s expansion

The communities that filled out the northeast and southeast. Standard rectangular trunk with round branches, twenty to forty years of loading, and very often a basement developed at some point along the way.

Everything built since

The new outer ring, where the systems are high-efficiency and sealed, the blowers are ECM, HRVs are standard, and the main problem is construction debris the builder never removed.

All three need a different approach, and knowing which one you are standing in front of before starting is most of the job.

What People Actually Tell Us

The thing that comes up most often in reviews is not the quality of the cleaning, which people largely have to take on trust. It is the pricing and the lack of pressure.

The price does not move

Quoted on the phone, same on the invoice. No mid-job discovery, no per-vent adjustment, no charge for the furnace being in an awkward spot. If we underestimate, that is our error to absorb.

Every upsell trick we do not use is listed here, along with why each one exists.

You see what came out

Before we pack up, you get a look inside the system and at the debris that came out of it. Not a photograph of somebody else's ductwork, not a stock image of mould. Yours.

It is the most convincing thing we can do and it costs us nothing, which makes it strange that it is unusual.

We say no

If you call and describe a house that does not need cleaning yet, we will tell you to wait a couple of years. That loses us the booking and gains us the call later.

No furnace sales pitch

We do not sell furnaces and have no affiliate arrangement with anyone who does. If we see something concerning in your heat exchanger we will tell you plainly and leave you to get it assessed by whoever you choose.

Where We Are and Where We Go

The shop is at 2121 39 Ave NE, Calgary. That puts us minutes from most of the northeast, which is why NE Calgary communities like Saddle Ridge, Taradale, Coventry Hills and Redstone make up a large share of the work.

We cover the whole city regardless. SE Calgary, NW Calgary and SW Calgary all get the same flat pricing with no travel charge, and we run out to Chestermere, Langdon, Airdrie and Strathmore regularly, usually grouped so nobody pays a premium for the distance.
