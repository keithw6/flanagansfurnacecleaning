Furnace and Duct Cleaning for Panorama Hills Homes

Panorama Hills built out from around 1999 through to the mid 2010s, spreading north from Country Hills Boulevard along Panatella and Panamount up toward Stoney Trail. It sits on the north edge of the city with views out over the valley, which is where the name comes from and why the lots step down in places.

The housing is noticeably larger than in the neighbouring communities. Where a Taradale or Saddle Ridge home might have twenty vents, a Panamount or Panatella two-storey commonly has thirty to forty, and the bigger executive homes on the ridge can run higher still. Several have a second furnace serving the upper floor or a bonus room over the garage.

None of that carries a surcharge with us. But it does mean a rough vent count genuinely helps us quote accurately, and it is the reason we ask rather than working from square footage alone.

What Panorama Hills Systems Involve

Two furnaces, sometimes

A second unit serving the upper floor or a bonus room is common in the larger homes. That means two blowers, two filter racks and two trunk systems, so it is a materially bigger job than a single-system house of similar footprint. We would rather establish that on the phone than discover it on the day.

Long vertical runs

Three levels plus a developed basement means duct runs with real height to them. More surface for dust to settle on, and more distance for the blower to push against. Loading tends to show up first as poor airflow in the furthest upstairs bedroom.

Walkouts and stepped lots

The ridge streets have walkout and side-split configurations, which produce duct layouts that are not obvious from the mechanical room. We map the system before starting so nothing gets missed.

Air conditioning

Far more common here than in older north Calgary stock. The evaporator coil sits directly in the airflow above the furnace and restricts air exactly the way a dirty filter does. Worth a look while we have the unit open.

Bonus rooms over garages

Notoriously difficult to heat evenly, and the complaint usually blamed on the furnace. Sometimes it genuinely is a loaded duct run. Often it is an insulation or balancing issue instead, and we will tell you which one it looks like rather than selling you a cleaning that will not fix it.

Exposed north edge

Panorama Hills sits high with open land to the north. Wind carries fine dust, and homes here take more of it than sheltered inner-city streets do.

A Note on Which Quadrant This Is

Panorama Hills technically sits in the northwest by Calgary's quadrant lines, on the boundary with the northeast. We have listed it under our NE Calgary service area because that is how we route to it and because it neighbours Coventry Hills and Country Hills far more closely than it does anywhere in the deep northwest.

If you are looking at the wider northwest, the NW Calgary page covers Evanston, Sage Hill, Nolan Hill, Tuscany, Edgemont and the rest. Same pricing either way, and no travel charge in any direction.
