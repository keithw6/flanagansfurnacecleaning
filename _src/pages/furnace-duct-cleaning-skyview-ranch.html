Furnace and Duct Cleaning for Skyview Ranch Homes

Skyview Ranch built out between roughly 2010 and 2020, filling the area between Métis Trail and 60 Street NE either side of Country Hills Boulevard. The oldest homes are now around fifteen years old, which puts a good share of the community squarely into its first proper cleaning cycle.

The community was planned at higher density than its neighbours, so it has a genuine mix: four-storey apartment condo buildings, extensive townhome complexes, semi-detached and laned product, and detached streets. That mix matters because a lot of companies quote one residential price regardless of what you actually live in, and a condo owner ends up subsidising a two-storey.

Higher density also means more households sharing walls, more people per acre, and in the multi-family buildings a set of shared systems that individual owners have no visibility into at all.

What We Find in Skyview Ranch Systems

Apartment condo units

Often a compact high-efficiency furnace or a hydronic air handler in a utility closet, feeding a handful of vents. Small, quick, and inexpensive to do. What owners rarely realise is that the makeup air and corridor pressurisation systems are the building's responsibility, not theirs. We will tell you where the line falls.

Townhome complexes

Eight to fourteen vents, furnace in a closet, short vertical runs. We regularly do several units in a complex in one visit, which is more efficient for everyone. If your board or a group of neighbours wants to coordinate, that is easy to arrange.

Laned and semi-detached

Narrow, deep, trunk running front to back. Rear-lane access means more grit tracked in from the lane and back yard than a front-driveway home gets, and that grit ends up in the cold air return.

Under the airport approach

Skyview Ranch sits close to the Calgary airport lands with a lot of open ground to the north and east. Spring and fall wind carries fine prairie dust, and open windows in shoulder season bring a steady supply of it indoors.

HRVs, largely untouched

Standard across this build era. Separate ducting, a core and two filters. In townhomes and condos the HRV is often in an awkward closet corner and has not been opened since the day it was installed.

Higher occupancy

Skyview Ranch has a lot of young families and multi-generational households in relatively compact homes. More cooking, more laundry and more foot traffic per square foot means the return side loads faster than the floor area suggests.

Landlords, Boards and Multiple Units

A large share of Skyview Ranch is rented, and a fair amount of our work here is for owners between tenancies or property managers dealing with several units at once.

Doing multiple units in one visit is straightforward and sensible. The equipment is already on site, so units after the first are quicker and priced accordingly. If you own or manage several doors in the same complex, tell us how many when you call and we will quote the group rather than each one separately.

Dryer vent cleaning is worth mentioning specifically for rentals. It takes under an hour, costs very little as an add-on, and matters a great deal if there is ever an insurance question about a laundry fire. Between tenancies is the natural time.

For whole buildings, common systems or corridor makeup air, that is commercial work and we quote it from a walk of the building.
