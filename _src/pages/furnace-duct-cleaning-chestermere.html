Three Kinds of Chestermere House

The old lakefront

Chestermere started as a summer lake community, and a lot of the housing closest to the water began as seasonal cottages that were winterised, extended and rebuilt over decades. Duct systems in these places are often a patchwork: an original small system with additions from two or three separate renovations, sometimes with runs that no longer serve anything.

These take longer than the square footage suggests because the system has to be mapped before it can be cleaned.

The newer subdivisions

Westmere, Rainbow Falls, Kinniburgh, Dawson's Landing and the rest of the growth since the early 2000s. Standard modern construction: sealed high-efficiency furnaces, HRVs in the newer builds, flex duct on some upper runs, and construction debris in anything built in the last few years.

Kinniburgh and Dawson's Landing in particular have homes young enough that drywall dust is still the main thing in the ducts.

Acreages and country residential

The land around Chestermere has a lot of country residential. Larger homes, longer duct runs, sometimes two furnaces, and often a gravel driveway and unpaved surroundings that put considerably more grit into the return than a town lot does.

Acreage homes generally suit a shorter cleaning cycle for exactly that reason.

What Living Beside Open Land Does

Chestermere sits east of Calgary surrounded by farmland, and that changes the dust load in a way people who move out from the city notice within a year.

Field dust and wind

Spring seeding and autumn harvest put a genuine amount of fine soil into the air, and there is nothing between the fields and your house to slow the wind down. It comes in on shoes, through open windows and through fresh air intakes.

Homes on the eastern and southern edges of town take the most of it.

Gravel and unpaved access

Acreage driveways, rural approaches and lanes generate grit continuously in dry weather. That grit tracks into the house and ends up in the cold air return, which is the part of the system that loads fastest anyway.

If you are on gravel, a three year cycle usually suits better than five.

Filters matter more here than in town for the same reason. A thin builder-grade filter changed twice a year is not enough on an acreage. We will show you what your rack takes and whether a thicker media cabinet would fit, which is covered in more detail on the indoor air quality page.
