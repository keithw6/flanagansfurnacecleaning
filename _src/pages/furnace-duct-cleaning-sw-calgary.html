What We Find in Southwest Systems

Large older homes

Mount Royal, Elbow Park, Britannia, Rideau Park and Pump Hill contain some of the biggest and oldest houses in the city. Several have been renovated repeatedly, and the ductwork tells that story: an original trunk with branches added in three different decades, sometimes a second furnace added for an upper floor or a converted attic.

Systems like this take longer and need mapping before we start, because the return path is rarely obvious and there is usually at least one run somebody forgot about. We quote from a proper vent count rather than square footage for exactly this reason.

As in the older northwest, asbestos-wrapped duct sections turn up occasionally on pre-1980 systems. We identify and leave them alone, and tell you plainly what you are looking at.

The hillside west

Aspen Woods, Springbank Hill, Signal Hill, Cougar Ridge, West Springs and Discovery Ridge are built across slope, so walkouts, split levels and three-storey duct runs are normal. Homes are large, vent counts of thirty to forty-five are common, and many have two furnaces.

Two furnaces means two of everything: two blowers, two filter racks, two trunk systems. It is a bigger job than a single-system house of the same footprint, and we would rather tell you that on the phone than discover it on the day.

Air conditioning is common here too, so the evaporator coil is worth a look while we are in the unit.

Infill, everywhere

The inner southwest has more infill construction than anywhere else in Calgary. If you have bought a new build slotted into an established street in Altadore, Killarney, South Calgary or Marda Loop, the system is modern and sealed but almost certainly still carrying drywall dust and sawdust from the build. That is a different job from cleaning the 1955 bungalow next door, and priced the same way.

Scheduling and Distance

Our shop is at 2121 39 Ave NE, so the southwest is the furthest corner of the city from us, generally thirty-five to forty-five minutes. It makes no difference to your price and there is no travel charge, but we do group southwest work where possible.

If you are flexible on which day, we can usually offer a morning slot. If you need a fixed date, tell us when you call and we will make it work.

Because vent counts run high in this quadrant, a rough count really does help us quote accurately. If you have no idea, tell us the square footage, the number of storeys, whether there is a walkout, and whether you think there are one or two furnaces.
