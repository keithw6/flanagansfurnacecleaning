Furnace and Duct Cleaning for Auburn Bay Homes

Auburn Bay built out between roughly 2007 and 2018 around its lake, bounded by Deerfoot, Stoney Trail and 52 Street SE, with the South Health Campus on its edge. Unlike Mahogany and Seton next door, it is essentially complete. No active construction phases, no framing on the next street, no grading dust blowing across the community.

That makes Auburn Bay a straightforward call. Homes here are between eight and eighteen years old, so the construction debris left by the builder has had years of ordinary household dust settle on top of it. Both layers are still in there, and neither is going anywhere on its own.

It is also old enough that basements developed after possession are common, and that is the event that most reliably puts a large volume of fine drywall dust through a return air system in a matter of days.

What Auburn Bay Systems Involve

Two layers of dust

Drywall dust and sawdust from the build, with eight to eighteen years of skin cells, fabric fibre, pet hair and cooking residue on top. We frequently pull both out of the same branch line, and the construction layer is the one people did not know was there.

HRVs, mostly untouched

Standard in this build era. Separate ducting, a core and two filters. If yours has not been opened since possession, and in most Auburn Bay homes it has not, it is running well below its rated airflow.

Air conditioning

Much more common here than in older Calgary stock. The evaporator coil sits in the airflow above the furnace and restricts air exactly as a dirty filter does. Worth a look while the unit is open, and it costs nothing to check.

Flex on upper runs

Insulated flexible duct on final runs to upper-floor registers appears in several of the builder products here. Ribbed interior holds dust, and an aggressive brush tears it. We identify what you have before starting.

Sealed high-efficiency furnaces

Two-stage and modulating condensing units with ECM blowers. Good equipment, but tighter tolerances mean restricted airflow shows up as a performance complaint faster than it would on older gear.

Developed basements

Frequently finished a few years after possession. If the furnace ran during drywall sanding, and it almost always did, that dust is through the whole return side. The most common reason we get called to this community.

Timing, and Why Auburn Bay Is Easier Than Its Neighbours

In Mahogany and Seton we often tell people to wait, because active construction on nearby streets means the system will simply reload. That advice does not apply here.

Auburn Bay is built out. Whatever is in your ductwork now is what will be there next year, plus a bit more. So if you have never had the system cleaned and you have been in the house more than a couple of years, it is due, and there is no reason to defer it.

After a first proper cleaning, three to five years is a sensible cycle for most households here. Pets, allergies or a basement development push you toward three. We will give you a straight recommendation based on what actually comes out of your system rather than a schedule that suits us.
