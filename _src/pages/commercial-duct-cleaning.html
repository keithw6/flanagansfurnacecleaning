How Commercial Work Differs From Residential

A house has one furnace, one trunk and twenty-odd vents. A commercial building might have four rooftop units feeding separate zones, long horizontal runs above a dropped ceiling, VAV boxes, and return air paths that use the ceiling plenum rather than dedicated ductwork.

That means the quote has to come from looking at the building. We walk the system, count the units and zones, find where the returns actually are, and check what access exists before giving you a number. A price quoted over the phone from a square footage figure is a guess, and guesses get revised upward mid-job.

Rooftop units

Most Calgary commercial buildings run rooftop package units. They pull outside air directly, which means they load faster than a residential furnace and they collect whatever is blowing across the roof. Access, safety and weather all factor into scheduling.

Zones and dropped ceilings

Long horizontal runs above tile ceilings need access panels or tile removal, done carefully and put back properly. We plan the access route before we start so we are not lifting tiles at random across your office.

Working around occupancy

Most of this work happens after close, overnight or at a weekend. That is normal for us and it is not billed at a premium. The building needs to be usable Monday morning, which means we clean up fully rather than leaving equipment staged.

Who Calls Us

Property managers

Buildings that have never been done, or where a tenant has raised air quality as an issue. We can provide before and after documentation for your records and for the tenant conversation.

Multi-unit residential is common work for us, including in-suite systems done unit by unit on a schedule that suits your residents.

Restaurants and food service

Kitchen environments load a ventilation system faster than anything else, and grease-laden air behaves differently from ordinary dust. Health inspections and insurance reviews both tend to prompt a call.

Note we clean HVAC ductwork. Kitchen exhaust hood and grease duct cleaning to fire-code standard is a separate specialist trade, and we will tell you plainly if that is what you actually need.

Daycares, clinics and schools

Places where parents and patients ask questions about air quality, and where the answer needs to be more than reassurance. These jobs get scheduled around closures and holidays.

Shops, warehouses and offices

Anywhere with a make-up air unit, an overhead heater, or a system that has been running since the building went up without anyone thinking about it. Industrial and shop environments load quickly with fine particulate.

What a Commercial Quote Includes

We come out, walk the system with you, and give you a written number covering the whole scope. That number holds. There is no discovery clause, no per-diffuser add-on, and no revision because a unit turned out to be dirtier than expected.

  • All supply ductwork per zone, trunk and branches
  • All return air paths, including plenum returns where used
  • Diffusers and grilles removed, cleaned and refitted
  • Air handler or rooftop unit interior, blower and cabinet
  • Filter replacement, with filter cost itemised separately so you can see it
  • Full clean-up and reinstatement of any ceiling access

Recurring schedules are worth setting up if your building loads quickly. Most offices are fine on three to five years. Kitchens, shops and buildings with heavy foot traffic are better annually or biennially. We will recommend an interval based on what we find, not a contract length that suits us.
