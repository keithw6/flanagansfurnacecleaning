Pricing and Booking

How much does furnace and duct cleaning cost in Calgary?

It depends on the size of the home and roughly how many vents it has. Call with the square footage, the number of storeys and whether the basement is developed, and you will have a firm number in about two minutes. There is no inspection fee and no site visit needed to get a price. Full ranges are on our pricing page.

Will you charge extra if my house has more vents than you estimated?

No. The number we quote on the phone covers the whole system however many vents turn up. If we misjudge the count, that is our estimating error and not a line on your invoice. Per-vent surcharges added after the fact are one of the most common complaints in this industry and we do not use them.

What is included in the price?

Every supply and return line, all registers removed and cleaned, the cold air return back to the furnace, the blower compartment and blower wheel, the burner compartment, a new standard filter, floor protection and full clean-up. Travel anywhere in Calgary is included. So is answering your questions for as long as you want to ask them.

Do you charge extra for travel?

Not anywhere in Calgary. We are based at 2121 39 Ave NE so the northeast is closest, but a job in the deep southwest is priced the same as one ten minutes away. For Chestermere, Langdon, Airdrie and Strathmore we group trips so nobody pays a premium for the distance.

Do you work evenings or weekends?

Saturdays yes, and evenings by arrangement. Commercial work is very often done after hours so the space stays usable, and we do not charge a premium for that. Sundays we are closed.

What payment do you take?

Cash, debit, credit card and e-transfer, due on completion. You get an itemised invoice.

The Work Itself

How long does a furnace and duct cleaning take?

Between two and four hours for most homes. A condo or small bungalow sits at the short end, a two-storey with a developed basement and thirty-plus vents at the long end. We do not compress a job to fit another one in behind it.

Do I need to be home?

Someone needs to let us in and we do prefer you are there at the end, because showing you what came out of the system is part of the job. Beyond that you are welcome to get on with your day.

Will it be messy?

It should not be. The system is put under negative pressure before anything is disturbed, so debris travels toward the vacuum rather than into your rooms. We put drop sheets at the entry, cover or remove boots, and route equipment so it is not dragging across floors. Anything we move gets put back.

Do you move furniture?

Light items to reach a vent, yes, that is included. We will not shift a piano or a loaded china cabinet, so if a vent is behind something substantial it helps to move it before we arrive. Tell us on the phone if access is going to be difficult anywhere.

How do I know the work was actually done?

Because you look at it. Before we pack up you get a look inside the system and at the debris that came out of your house, not a photograph of somebody else's ductwork. It is the most convincing thing we can do and it costs us nothing, which makes it odd that it is unusual.

What Your Home Actually Needs

How often should I have my furnace and ducts cleaned?

Most Calgary homes are well served every three to five years. Pets, allergies or a recent renovation push you toward three. A quiet two-person household with no pets can comfortably go five or more. If you call and describe a house that does not need us yet, we will tell you to wait.

Do I need both the furnace and the ducts done?

Usually yes, because they are one connected system. Cleaning the ducts while leaving a dust-loaded blower wheel means the furnace feeds dust straight back into clean pipe. Cleaning the furnace while leaving the ducts means it pulls settled dust off the duct walls within a few cycles. Doing both at once costs far less than two separate visits.

My house is brand new. Do I need it done?

Eventually, but not the week you move in. New homes are full of drywall dust, sawdust and offcuts that the builder walkthrough rarely deals with properly, so it does need doing. We suggest waiting about a year after possession, and until any basement development is finished, so one cleaning does the whole job instead of two doing half each. The exception is asthma, significant allergies or a newborn in the house, in which case book it as soon as the trades are done.

I just developed my basement. Should I clean before or after?

After, always. Drywall sanding puts an enormous volume of fine dust into the return air side, and if the furnace ran at all during the work it went right through the system. Cleaning first and then developing means paying twice.

Do I need my ducts sanitized?

Almost certainly not. Dust in ductwork is skin cells, fabric fibre, dander and soil. It is not alive and it does not grow. Mould needs moisture, and a dry Calgary duct system on a working furnace does not have it. Sanitizing is genuinely warranted after water damage, a rodent or bird problem, smoke damage, or where we find actual moisture, and we will show you the problem before recommending it. If someone quoted you sanitizing without showing you anything, be sceptical.

Can duct cleaning help with allergies?

It helps, but let us be honest about how much. Cleaning removes the reservoir of dust that the system keeps recirculating, which is a real improvement. It is not a treatment for allergies and anyone promising that is overselling. Pairing it with a better filter and a working humidifier does more than cleaning alone, and we will talk you through both.

Other Services and Situations

Do you sell furnaces?

No, and we have no arrangement with anyone who does. That matters, because it means when we look at your heat exchanger we have nothing to gain from finding a problem. If we see something concerning we will tell you plainly and leave you to have it assessed by whoever you choose.

Do you do condos and townhouses?

Yes. Smaller systems, fewer vents, priced accordingly. Some buildings need the work arranged through the condo board or property manager, particularly where roof access is involved, and we deal with that regularly.

What about dryer vents?

We clean those too, and more people should have it done. Lint packs along the pipe walls, airflow drops, the dryer runs longer and hotter, and the pipe is lined with the most flammable material in the house. It is cheapest as an add-on while we are already there for a furnace and duct cleaning.

Do you clean central vacuum systems?

Yes, including VACUFLO systems and every other brand. Blocked tubing cleared, canisters and filters serviced, suction tested at each inlet. We are a cleaning and service company rather than a dealership, so if your unit needs warranty work we will point you to an authorised dealer instead of voiding it.
