Furnace and Duct Cleaning for McKenzie Towne Homes

McKenzie Towne started in the mid 1990s and built out through to around 2010, laid out on New Urbanist principles: a proper high street at Elgin, front porches close to the sidewalk, rear lanes, garages at the back, and lots noticeably narrower and deeper than the Calgary norm.

That design decision, made thirty years ago by planners, is why the duct cleaning here is different. When a lot is narrow, the house goes up and back rather than out. The trunk runs front to back along the length of the basement with short branches off it, the runs to upper floors are tall, and the mechanical room is squeezed against a party wall or into a corner under the stairs.

Nothing about that makes it harder to do properly, but it does mean equipment has to be set up thoughtfully, and it means some companies quietly charge an access fee. We do not. The house is the house.

What the McKenzie Towne Layout Means for Your System

Tall vertical runs

Three levels on a narrow footprint means duct runs with real height. The far upstairs bedroom is the first place a partially loaded system shows up as poor airflow, and it is usually blamed on the furnace rather than the pipe.

Cramped mechanical rooms

Furnaces tucked under stairs, behind laundry, or in a closet with barely enough clearance to open the blower door. It slows setup down but it is part of the job, not an extra.

Rear-lane living

Detached garages off a gravel lane mean people come and go across unpaved ground and a back yard rather than a paved front drive. More grit at the back door means more grit in the cold air return.

Developed basements

Very common here, both original and added later. Where the basement was finished after possession with the furnace running, that drywall dust went straight through the return side and is still in the system unless it has been cleaned since.

Twenty to thirty years of loading

The earliest streets are approaching thirty years old. That is a genuine volume of accumulated household dust, pet hair and cooking residue, and it is where the before and after is most obvious.

Condos and townhomes at Prestwick

McKenzie Towne includes substantial multi-family sections. Smaller systems, eight to fourteen vents, priced well below a detached home. Several units in one visit is straightforward if neighbours want to coordinate.

A Word on Dryer Vents Here

Narrow houses with laundry on an upper floor produce some of the longest dryer vent runs we deal with, often terminating through the roof. Long runs with multiple bends give lint far more surface to collect on, and a rooftop termination is the one nobody ever checks.

If your clothes need two cycles, or the laundry room gets hot when the dryer runs, that is the reason. It is cheapest done as an add-on while we are already there. More on dryer vent cleaning.
